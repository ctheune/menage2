0.1
---

Todos, checklists, checklist lines, runs and ingredients became one kind of
thing with five shapes.

- They share a base table. Text, notes, tags, assignees, owner, status,
  due date and recurrence live there; each kind keeps only what is its own.

- Files and links hang off any of them, so an ingredient can carry a photo
  of the packaging and a checklist line can carry a link. The tag picker
  and the assignee field are the same ones everywhere.

- A protocol run and the todo that said it was due were one thing written
  twice. They are one row now.

- Tags are rows. Renaming one is an update to that row rather than a
  rewrite of everything carrying it, and the vocabulary is one table rather
  than four arrays and a comma-separated string.

- An assignee is a reference to a user or a team, so a name that addresses
  nobody cannot be stored. Users and teams share one namespace, enforced by
  the database rather than by two admin views remembering to check.
